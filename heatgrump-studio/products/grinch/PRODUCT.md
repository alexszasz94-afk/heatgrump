# Grinch (HeatGrump) heated robe — everything Claude needs

## What it is
Long hooded heated robe in Grinch colours: deep-red plush top and long sleeves, black belt, scalloped red/green line with thin gold piping, long olive-green plush lower part down to the floor, white fluffy sherpa trim on the front edges and the hem, bright shaggy lime-green faux-fur cuffs over white sherpa cuffs, red Santa-hat hood with a white pompom whose FRONT panel is green with the grumpy face (yellow eyes) — the face is ONLY on the front of the hood. Inside: red top, green bottom (never white). Inside the hem: two green foot pockets with a white fluffy band on top, joined in the middle (W shape). Comes with a white oval remote (5 green lights) and a grey cable connector.
