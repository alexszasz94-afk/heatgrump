# Robe Studio — start here

**This setup is only for Claude Code on the web (claude.ai/code).** Everything runs in your browser. Nothing to install, no experience needed. The step-by-step page with every click explained is **SETUP.html**.

**You never edit anything** — no video editing, no files, no code. You send a reel or an idea; Claude makes, checks and edits the whole video and gives you the finished MP4.

**Who does what**
- **Alex** prepares the kit and sends you: this guide and a folder **robe-studio-upload** with 11 files (the whole kit: `CLAUDE.md` + 10 pieces). About once a week he sends a small update file.
- **You** make the accounts (Claude Pro, Higgsfield, free GitHub), click the 5 steps below once, then just send Claude reels and ideas and post the videos.

**Once (about 5 minutes)**
1. On **github.com** (free account): **+** → **New repository** → name `robe-studio` → **Private** → **Create repository** → click **uploading an existing file** → open Alex's folder, select the 11 files inside (Ctrl+A / Cmd+A) and drag the files (not the folder) → **Commit changes**.
2. Go to **claude.ai/customize/connectors** → **Higgsfield** → **Connect** → log in to Higgsfield.
3. Go to **claude.ai/code** → connect GitHub if asked → pick your `robe-studio` repository.
4. Click the environment name at the top → **Edit** → **Network access** → either **Full** (easiest) or **Custom/Limited** with the list from `setup/allowed-domains.txt` pasted in **Allowed domains** (stricter) → **Save**.
5. Write **"Hi, set me up"**. Claude does the rest and tells you when it's ready.

**Every day**
Just talk to Claude like to a person:
- "Fă asta cu Grinch: <link de reel>"
- "I have an idea: she opens the door and her mom is holding a giant gift box…"
- "Use another song" · "Cozy: hot cocoa on the sofa" · "How many videos can I still make?"

Want a specific song? Drag the MP3 into the chat and say "use this song" — Claude puts its big moment on the cut after the hook.

**Updates:** when Alex sends `robe-studio-update-<date>.zip`, drag it into a chat and write "here is the new update from Alex". Claude installs it.

Claude makes the video (classic cozy ending, or tell it another one), checks it and gives you the MP4 + a caption + the text to add in CapCut. Your part is the ideas: copy reels that work, and invent your own hooks.
