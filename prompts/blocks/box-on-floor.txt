THE BOX IS ON THE FLOOR — ABSOLUTE RULE, HIGHEST PRIORITY: the opened box stands ON THE FLOOR of the room, its whole bottom resting flat on the floor, with the floor clearly visible around and under it. It is NEVER floating in the air, NEVER held up, NEVER on a table, counter, bed, sofa or any piece of furniture. The person bends forward or kneels to reach into it, so their hands can be inside the box while it sits on the floor. The box's shadow and contact with the floor must be visible and believable.
