LIGHT (permanent rule): very soft, very natural light. Diffuse daylight from a window and/or the warm glow of real lamps and string lights; gentle, low-contrast, wrap-around light with soft shadow edges and smooth highlights; true, slightly warm colours. Never harsh, never hard direct light, no spotlight, no dramatic contrast, no HDR, no glow/bloom halos, no glossy CGI look, no over-sharpening. Looks like a real, unedited phone photo or video taken in a cozy home.
