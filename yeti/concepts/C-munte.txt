CONCEPT C — MOUNTAIN YETI. Upper body and sleeves in snow-white plush. The lower skirt is deep slate-blue plush with a pieced snowy MOUNTAIN RANGE rising from the hem: white peaks with soft icy-blue shadows, the tallest peak reaching mid-thigh. White sherpa trim down the front edges, around the hood and along the hem. Sleeves end in shaggy snow-white Yeti-fur cuffs. Lining: slate-blue minky. Pockets: plain slate-blue plush with a white sherpa band on top.
THE HOOD: a rounded white Yeti head. Two short curved icy-blue horns on top. Small rounded ears on the sides. On the front above the brim, a grumpy Yeti face: pale icy-blue skin, narrow grumpy eyes, heavy shaggy white eyebrows, small dark nose. A tuft of shaggy white fur on the crown between the horns.
