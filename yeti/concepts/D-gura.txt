CONCEPT D — YETI MOUTH HOOD. Robe body in snow-white plush with a smooth pale icy-blue BELLY panel on the front that forms one rounded Yeti tummy when the robe is closed. Sleeves end in shaggy white Yeti-fur cuffs. The two foot pockets at the hem are shaped like big Yeti feet with three grey toe pads. Lining: icy-blue minky. Pockets: plain white plush.
THE HOOD: the whole hood IS the Yeti's head and the wearer's face peeks out of the Yeti's OPEN MOUTH. The hood brim is the Yeti's mouth: a soft white sherpa upper lip with a row of small soft white felt fangs, and a soft lower lip under the chin. ABOVE the mouth, on the top front of the hood: two big grumpy Yeti eyes with heavy shaggy white eyebrows, a small dark nose, pale icy-blue skin around them. Two short curved grey horns on top, small rounded ears on the sides. Shaggy white fur covers the rest of the head.
