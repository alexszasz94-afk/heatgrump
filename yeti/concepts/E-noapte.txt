CONCEPT E — MIDNIGHT YETI. Upper body and sleeves in deep midnight-navy plush; lower skirt in long-pile shaggy snow-white Yeti fur. The line between them is a soft wavy snowdrift edge with thin silver piping. Wide white sherpa trim down both front edges, around the hood and along the hem. Sleeves end in shaggy snow-white Yeti-fur cuffs. Lining: icy-blue minky. Pockets: plain midnight-navy plush with a white sherpa band on top.
THE HOOD: a rounded shaggy snow-white Yeti head. Two short curved icy-blue horns on top. Small rounded ears on the sides. On the front above the brim, a grumpy Yeti face: pale icy-blue skin, narrow grumpy eyes, heavy white eyebrows, small dark nose, one tiny fang.
