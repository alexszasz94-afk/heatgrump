CONCEPT A — GLACIER YETI. Upper body and sleeves in icy glacier-blue plush; lower skirt in snow-white plush. The line between them is shaped like a row of hanging ICICLES (pointed drips of blue over white) edged with thin silver piping. Wide white sherpa trim down both front edges, around the hood opening and along the bottom hem. Sleeves end in long-pile shaggy snow-white Yeti-fur cuffs. Lining: icy glacier-blue minky. Pockets: plain snow-white plush with a sherpa band on top.
THE HOOD: a rounded Yeti head in icy glacier-blue plush, no long tip. Two short curved white horns on top, one above each temple. Two small rounded ears on the sides. On the front panel above the brim, a grumpy Yeti face: pale icy-blue skin, two narrow sleepy-grumpy eyes, heavy shaggy white eyebrows, small dark nose, one tiny white fang. A shaggy white fur tuft runs from the forehead back over the crown.
