Barbie Heated Wheel 💗

Warm hands on frozen mornings. Pink quilted leather, hot-pink script, a little pink tag, three heat levels and no cord hanging from your wheel.

🔥

Heats in 2–3 minutes

Heating wires sit under the grips at 10 & 2 o'clock, right where your hands go.

🔌

No dangling cord

Runs on any USB power bank hidden in the back pocket. The wheel turns freely.

🎚️

3 heat levels

One button on the front. Auto-off after 45 min for safety.

🚗

Fits most cars & SUVs

Universal 15 in (37–38 cm) with a stretchy elastic edge.

How to put it on

Takes about one minute. No tools.

1

Charge it up

Flip the cover, open the back pocket and plug in your power bank. Zip it closed.

2

Put it on

Script facing you, hook the top over the wheel, then pull the elastic down and around the bottom. The button ends up at the bottom front.

3

Press & get cozy

Press the button to switch on, press again to change the heat level.

Heat levels

Low~40 °C · 104 °F
Medium~45 °C · 113 °F
High~50 °C · 122 °F
