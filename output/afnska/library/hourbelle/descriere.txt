A winter coat cut like a gown. Hourbelle is a long quilted puffer with a corset-shaped bodice that nips in at your waist and a full bell skirt that swings when you walk - warm like a puffer, shaped like a dress.
Corset-shaped bodice with princess seams and a waist seam that shows your waist
Corset lacing at the back of the waist
Double-breasted front with fabric-covered buttons and a tall stand-up collar
Full, flared, ankle-length skirt with diamond quilting
Light padded fill, matte outer with a soft sheen
Flap pockets on the hips
Care: hand wash in cold water or a gentle cycle in a laundry bag, hang to dry, do not tumble dry.
