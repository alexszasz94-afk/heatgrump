Questions

Before you buy.

Does it really float?

Yes. A magnet in the car's floor is held by electromagnets in the base, and sensors keep adjusting the field so the car stays centred and turning. You can pass your hand through the gap.

Is it hard to set up?

No. Plug in the base, hold the car over the centre, lower it slowly until you feel it catch, and let go. The quick-start card shows each step. It takes about a minute, and the first try can take a few attempts.

What happens if the power goes off?

The car gently drops onto the base. Hold it up, switch the power back on, and place it again.

Can I leave it on all day?

Yes, it is made for continuous display. Keep it away from other strong magnets, phones and bank cards, and on a stable, level surface.

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave within 24–48 hours with tracking. You get the tracking link by email as soon as it ships.

What if I don't love it?

Send it back within 30 days for a full refund. Just reply to your order email and we'll take it from there.
